Early experiment, not a navigation or boating instrument. This arrow points toward a coordinate as the crow flies. It does not identify trails, obstacles, private property, safe water, or a walkable route. Never follow it into water or off an established trail. Phone-camera AR here is a 2D overlay; accurate world-anchored 3D graphics are planned for a later native ARCore version. Camera, GPS, saved spots, and community-demo activity are processed locally; no location or community information is uploaded by this prototype.
